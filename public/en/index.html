How it works

  1. Message us. Tell us what you want engraved, the size, and the wood you prefer.
  2. Approve the 3D render. We send a 3D render of your piece. Nothing gets cut until you say yes.
  3. We carve and finish. CNC carving, hand sanding, then imported oils and orange wax.
  4. We ship it. Guaranteed delivery with Servientrega or Tramaco Express. Shipping cost depends on your city and we confirm it before you pay.

Materials and finish

Plywood, laurel, and cedar or teak on request. We also cut melamine, acrylic vinyl and cintra. Food-contact boards and trays are solid wood, finished with imported food-safe oils and orange wax.

Frequently asked questions

How much do your pieces cost?

Cutting boards start at $7, decorative trays at $15 and custom vintage signs at $30. The final price depends on size, wood and design, and we confirm it on WhatsApp before we start. Orders of 5 or more pieces get a special price.

What wood do you use?

We work with plywood and laurel, and cedar or teak on request. We also cut melamine, acrylic vinyl and cintra. Boards and trays that touch food are made from solid wood (laurel, cedar or teak).

Are your cutting boards and trays food safe?

Serving boards and trays are made from solid wood and finished with imported food-safe oils and orange wax. Decorative plywood pieces are for display only, not for food.

Can I see the design before you cut it?

Yes, always. Before we start the CNC machine we send you a 3D render of your piece, and we don't cut until you approve it.

What can you engrave?

Names, dates, short phrases, drawings, logos or vintage-style designs. Send us your idea on WhatsApp and we will turn it into a design for you to approve.

Where is your workshop?

In Riobamba, Ecuador, on Av. Antonio José de Sucre on the road to Guano, just past the Mall El Paseo Shopping.

Do you ship across Ecuador?

Yes. We ship with Servientrega or Tramaco Express to Cuenca, Quito, Guayaquil and the rest of the country, with guaranteed delivery. Shipping cost depends on the destination and we confirm it in your quote, before you pay. Allow about 5 business days from the moment you approve the design.
